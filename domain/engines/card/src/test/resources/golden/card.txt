Golden file for :domain:engines:card (issue 6.1; FR-ACC-002, RULE-CC-UTIL v1.0, RULE-CC-DUE v1.0).

Every record is one card on one day. Amounts are PAISE (MNY-001), never rupees — a fixture written
in rupees is a fixture that silently passes at a hundredth of the amount it claims. Ratios are
integer BASIS POINTS (MNY-002): 3500 is 35%, not 35.

The first block of records walks a single ₹2,00,000 card across a whole billing cycle, one
significant day at a time — statement day, mid-cycle, the reminder window opening, the due day, the
day after. That walk is what the acceptance criterion means by "a golden-file test on a billing
cycle": the alerts are not asserted one at a time in isolation, they are asserted as a sequence, so
a rule that fires a day early or stops a day late shows up as a diff rather than as a passing test
somewhere else. The records after it are the edges that walk cannot reach.

Record format — one `# key=value` per line, records separated by a line of `===`:

  today            ISO yyyy-MM-dd, the date being asked about (TIM-002; the engine reads no clock)
  limit            paise, the sanctioned credit limit — must be positive
  statement_day    1..31
  due_day          1..31
  last_statement   paise, the amount on the most recent statement (optional; absent = none recorded)
  minimum_due      paise (optional)
  outstanding      paise, what is owed right now, as a positive magnitude
  expect_statement ISO date, the cycle's statement date
  expect_due       ISO date, when that statement must be paid
  expect_days      whole days from `today` to the due date; negative once it has passed
  expect_live_bps  live utilisation in bps (outstanding / limit), or `none`
  expect_stmt_bps  statement utilisation in bps (last_statement / limit), or `none`
  expect_unbilled  paise, spend since the statement was cut, floored at zero
  expect_available paise, limit less outstanding, floored at zero
  expect_alerts    comma-separated CardAlertKind names in order, or `none`

Absent optional keys are unset, not zero — `last_statement` in particular, because a card with no
statement recorded has no statement utilisation, and rendering that as 0% would tell the user they
owe nothing (P-03).

The whole file is reproducible to the byte (P-08): every date is an argument and no clock is read.
