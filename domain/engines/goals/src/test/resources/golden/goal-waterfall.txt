Golden records for GoalWaterfallEngine — issue 7.3, SRS §15.1, FR-GOAL-003, FR-GOAL-005.

Every amount is PAISE (MNY-001), never rupees. Every record is reckoned from one fixed day,
2026-09-03, so this file is byte-reproducible and no record's expectation moves with the calendar.

These records run the REAL PIPELINE: each goal is projected by GoalEngine first, exactly as
GoalWaterfallRepository does it, and the waterfall then allocates across the projections. That is
deliberate. The levers need `remaining`, `monthsRemaining` and `saved`, and hand-writing those into
the fixture would duplicate GoalEngine's arithmetic in a file that could then quietly disagree with
it. GoalGoldenTest already gates the projection on its own; this file gates the composition.

WHAT THE ACCEPTANCE CRITERION ASKED FOR — "golden-file test on competing goals" — is records 2, 7
and 14: several goals asking for one surplus, and the order deciding who is short.

Record format. One record per `===` block, one `# key=value` per line:

  label             what the record is for; also the goal ids
  surplus           the month's surplus in paise, or `unknown` for null. May be negative.
  basis             OBSERVED_MEDIAN | DECLARED_ENVELOPE | NONE
  ef_topup          EmergencyFundPlan.topUpMonthly, in paise. Optional, defaults to 0.
  ef_runway_bps     EmergencyFundPlan.runwayMonthsBps — basis points of a MONTH, so 30000 is three
                    months (MNY-002). `unknown` for null. Optional, defaults to unknown.
  ef_gate_months    RULE-EMERG-FIRST.min_runway_months. Optional, defaults to 3.
  goal              repeatable: name|target|saved|target_date. Zero or more.
  expect_feasibility        FEASIBLE | INFEASIBLE | UNKNOWN
  expect_ef_applied         true | false — whether RULE-EMERG-FIRST held the goals at zero
  expect_ef_allocated       what the emergency fund took off the top
  expect_required           Σ requiredMonthly — §15.1's left-hand side
  expect_allocated          what the goals actually got
  expect_gap                required − allocated
  expect_unallocated        what was left after every claim
  expect_alloc              per-goal allocation, comma-separated, in order; `none` for no goals
  expect_levers             per-goal `ext|reduceTo|increase`, comma-separated; `-` for a fully
                            funded goal (no levers), `none` in a slot for a null lever, and `none`
                            for the whole field when there are no goals
