Golden records for OrderOfOperationsEngine — issue 7.5, SRS §36, AI-FOO.

Every amount is PAISE (MNY-001), never rupees: 5000000 is ₹50,000. Every rate is BASIS POINTS
(MNY-002): 3600 is 36%. A runway is basis points of a MONTH: 30000 is three months. Every record is
reckoned from one fixed day, 2026-09-17, so the file is byte-reproducible.

WHAT THE ACCEPTANCE CRITERION ASKED FOR — "golden-file test across representative states" — is the
whole file: a cold start, a young earner with card debt and a thin buffer, a gated emergency fund, a
household with nothing left to do, a grey-zone car loan, a home loan, a month in the red, an
unknown card rate on the band edges, an unsized fund, and both sides of RULE-EMERG-FIRST's gate by
one basis point.

EVERY EXPECTATION IS WRITTEN OUT, NEVER DERIVED. Issue 7.4's first golden assertion computed its
expectation with the same subtraction the engine performs, so editing a record moved input and
expectation together and the test stayed green. The arithmetic behind each record is shown in its
comment lines so a reviewer can check it by hand — but the test reads only the `# key=value` lines.

Record format. One record per `===` block, one `# key=value` per line; other lines are comments.

  label               what the record is for
  surplus             the month's surplus in paise, or `unknown`. May be negative.
  basis               OBSERVED_MEDIAN | DECLARED_ENVELOPE | NONE
  essentials          monthly essentials, or `unknown`. Optional, defaults to unknown.
  liquid              liquid funds. Optional, defaults to 0.
  ef_shortfall        the emergency fund's shortfall, or `unknown` (an unsized fund). Optional, unknown.
  ef_topup            AI-EMF's monthly pace. Optional, defaults to 0.
  ef_runway_bps       the runway, or `unknown`. Optional, defaults to unknown.
  debt                repeatable: id|CARD or LOAN|outstanding|apr_bps or `unknown`.
  goals_required      what every goal needs this month. Optional, defaults to 0.
  goal_count          how many active goals. Optional, defaults to 0.
  expect_top          the top action's stage, or `none`.
  expect_unallocated  what is left after every stage.
  expect              repeatable, exactly eight, in stage order:
                      STAGE|STATUS|REASON|need or `unknown`|amount
  expect_debts        optional, repeatable: STAGE|comma-separated debt ids in the order shown.
