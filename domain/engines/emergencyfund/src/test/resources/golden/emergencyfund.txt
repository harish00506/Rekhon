Golden file for :domain:engines:emergencyfund (issue 7.2; AI-EMF, RULE-EMF-MULT v1.0,
RULE-EMF-COACH v1.0, RULE-RUNWAY-M v1.0).

Every record is one assessment. Amounts are PAISE (MNY-001), never rupees — a fixture written in
rupees is a fixture that silently passes at a hundredth of the amount it claims. Ratios are integer
BASIS POINTS (MNY-002): 10 000 is 100%, and a runway of 15 000 is one and a half months.

Every record shares one `today`, 2026-09-02, so the whole file is reproducible to the byte (P-08) —
this engine reads no clock (TIM-001).

The status, the multiplier and the cv are asserted as well as the money, because a figure that came
out right from the wrong branch is exactly what a golden file exists to catch. Three pairs in this
file are indistinguishable by their amounts alone:

  * a top-up of 0 is correct for a FUNDED fund AND for a SURPLUS one (records 3 and 4);
  * a top-up of 0 is also correct for UNKNOWN, where there is no target to top up (record 5);
  * a null cv is correct for "too few months to measure" AND for "three months of no income at
    all" (records 8 and 9) — different branches of the same guard, same M, same everything else.

Records 10 to 13 sit exactly on RULE-EMF-MULT's band edges. The row's wording is `< cv_low_bps`
adds nothing and `<= cv_high_bps` adds the middle bump, so a cv of exactly 1 000 is already the
middle band and a cv of exactly 3 000 is still the middle band. Record 12 is the first cv the high
bump applies to. Moving either edge by one basis point must move exactly one of these four.

Note what the file does NOT contain: a record where `RULE-RUNWAY-M`'s clamp fires. With the shipped
params it cannot — 6 base months plus at most a 3-month volatility bump is 9, comfortably inside
[3, 12]. The clamp is exercised in EmergencyFundEngineTest with moved rules instead, which is the
honest place for it: pretending a golden record clamps would be a fixture asserting something the
shipped rulebook never does. See ADR-0034.

Record format — one `# key=value` per line, records separated by a line of `===`:

  essentials        paise a month, or `none` when the app cannot size it yet
  basis             OBSERVED_MEDIAN | DECLARED_ENVELOPE | NONE  (must be NONE iff essentials is none)
  incomes           paise per whole closed month, comma-separated, or `none` for no history
  liquid            paise that could be spent today (savings + cash)
  expect_cv         basis points of coefficient of variation, or `none` when unmeasurable
  expect_months     multiplier M after RULE-RUNWAY-M's clamp
  expect_target     paise, essentials x M
  expect_topup      paise, the largest monthly instalment that closes the shortfall
  expect_runway     basis points of a month that the liquid funds buy, or `none`
  expect_funded     basis points of the target that is funded; NOT capped at 10 000
  expect_status     SURPLUS | FUNDED | BUILDING | UNKNOWN | URGENT   (RULE-EMF-COACH)
