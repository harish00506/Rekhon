### What phase 7 must exercise

1. Three goals whose required monthlies together exceed the surplus → the banner reads infeasible
   **with the gap**, and the third goal shows all three levers.
2. Drag the third goal to the top → the allocations change and the trip is the one that goes short.
3. TalkBack (or the accessibility scanner) → "Move up" / "Move down" are announced on each card, and
   the first card offers only "Move down".
4. A profile with a runway under three months → the emergency-first line appears and every goal reads
   "Waiting on your emergency fund"; top the buffer up and it disappears.
5. A fresh profile → the card reads "not enough history yet", **not** "your goals do not fit".
6. Airplane mode → the whole screen still computes (P-04).
7. Dark mode, and 200% font on the plan card.
