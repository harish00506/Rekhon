any of them: (1) crossing 80% posts exactly one notification; (2) crossing 100% posts a second;
(3) further spending posts no third; (4) denying the permission leaves the banner correct and the
app alive; (5) all of the above in airplane mode (P-04); (6) dark mode and 200% font, with the
overspend **not** drawn in the money-in green — the exact defect the 4.4 device run found (`0e6aa41`).

once-per-band, has likewise only been written, never executed.
