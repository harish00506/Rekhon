## Notes for the reviewer

- **The device run found a defect no test could have**: the card editor (6.1) has no rate field, so the
  first build's "Add the rate in Accounts" button was a dead end. Removed, and recorded as a follow-up.
  Until that field exists every card ranks as fire debt — the right default for Indian cards, and said
  on screen.
- **Two readings of §36 to push on** (ADR-0037): the grey band runs to 13.5% rather than stopping at
  12%, and below `RULE-EMERG-FIRST`'s gate the emergency fund may take its whole shortfall.
- **A known divergence with 7.3**: past the gate, AI-FOO funds the emergency fund's pace before the goals;
  the goals screen's waterfall does not. Recorded as ADR-0037's first follow-up rather than fixed by
  reopening 7.3.
- **Nothing minted**: rulebook 1.15.0 and the FOO file 1.0 are both unchanged; `RULE-EMERG-FIRST`'s
  number still has exactly one mirror.
