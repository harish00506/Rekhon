## Notes for whoever picks this up next

- **`CLS-MER-011` was wrong and reading it found out.** Its bare literal `coin` filed a laundromat
  under Investment. Fixed as a data row at v1.1, id kept. If another short literal is added to the
  knowledge base, add its trap to `eval/categorisation.txt` in the same commit.
- **The eval set is deliberately not at 100 %.** `AMAZONPAY` and `BYJUS` are real descriptors this
  engine misses; both are labelled and left in. Do not "fix" the score by removing them — the fix is
  a knowledge-base row.
- **`UBER EATS` classifies as Transport** and is *not* in the eval set, because putting a known wrong
  answer in a zero-tolerance half would make the gate unrunnable. It is recorded in ENGINE.md's
  known limits instead.
- **Issue 4.3 is nature classification** (§8.3). The merchant rows carry `default_nature` and this
  engine deliberately ignores it — reading it would be a nature guessed from the merchant alone,
  which is wrong exactly where §8.3.1's first three steps matter (loan account, investment transfer,
  asset record).
