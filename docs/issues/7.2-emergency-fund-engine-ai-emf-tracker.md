### Corrections made to my own claims

- **The evidence drawer rendered a domain token.** "Counted as essential: NEED" reached a real
  screen. Every test passed: the engine's figure was right, the string was externalised, the lint
  detector was satisfied — and the sentence was still one no user could read. It is the same class
  of defect issue 7.1's drills found twice, and the same lesson: **no assertion about a figure
  catches a sentence.**

- The first golden file shipped record 4 with a placeholder verdict (`UNKNOWN_PLACEHOLDER` where
  `SURPLUS` belonged). Caught before the file was ever run.
- The repository test first asserted the evidence account names in **my** order rather than
  `observeAccounts`' own. The test was wrong, not the code; the assertion now states that the order
  is the repository's and has to stay stable (P-08).

### Still open

- **The three deferred multiplier terms** (dependents, health cover, job stability) need three
  profile fields before their params can be minted. Named in ADR-0034; their absence is asserted by
  `RulebookDriftTest`.
- **The per-account liquidity tier** §10.1 describes. Until it exists the runway is understated for
  anyone holding an FD or a liquid fund, and the screen says so. It has a second consumer waiting —
  `RULE-IDLE-CASH`'s detector (§11.2).
- **7.3's feasibility waterfall** consumes this engine's runway through `RULE-EMERG-FIRST`; out of
  scope here.
