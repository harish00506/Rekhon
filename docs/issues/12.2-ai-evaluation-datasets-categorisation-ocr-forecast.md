## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] Datasets are frozen and versioned; runners report accuracy vs thresholds. **Frozen and size-guarded already** — all four runners assert a `MIN_FIXTURES` floor, verified by gutting the categorisation set to 5 records and watching it fail. **Versioned by this issue:** each declares `# dataset-version:` in its header, and a set without one fails rather than reporting an unattributable score. **Reported by this issue:** every run prints score, counts, floor and revision — `categorisation v1.0 — accuracy 96.3% (53/55), floor 92% — ok`.
- [x] Regressions block merges in CI (§21.5). They already did, inside `unitTests`; `aiEval` now **names** the gate so a drop reads as "the receipt reader regressed" rather than as one failure among five thousand. The CI step is additional to `unitTests`, not a replacement, and is not `|| true`.
- [x] Extending a dataset is documented — `docs/testing/ai-evaluation-datasets.md`, including the three things **not** to do: relabel an awkward case to go green, lower a floor without an ADR, or regenerate the forecast ledgers until the numbers suit.
- [x] Golden rules respected. P-01 is served, not just unbroken: the guide states that no case may be drawn from real user data, and the forecast ledgers' own header records which shapes they model **and which they deliberately do not**, so the gate is not measuring the model against its own assumptions. MNY-001/002 hold — amounts stay paise, the forecast's bounds stay basis points, and that is exactly why the forecast keeps its own reporter.
- [x] All Skill Rules and label-specific rules below are satisfied.
- [x] Definition of Done met (`00-issue-workflow.md` steps 9-11; `/pre-merge`).

**What the survey corrected.** I first reported that none of the accuracy gates pinned its dataset size — so a set could be shrunk to a handful of easy cases to lift a score. Measuring disproved it: cutting categorisation from 76 records to 5 **fails**. My grep had missed the guards. Three of AC1's four words were already true, and the real gaps were versioning, reporting and naming.

**What reporting found on its first run.** `forecast-ledgers v1.0 — mean P10-P90 band coverage 7027 bps (min 7000) — ok`: **0.27% of margin**, invisible until something printed it. That is the argument for AC1's "report" in one line.

**No accuracy threshold was changed by this issue.** It reports and attributes; retuning a floor is a deviation needing its own ADR.

## Guiding Principles (CLAUDE.md §1 / design spec §2)

- **P-03 Numbers from math, words from AI** - engines compute every figure; the LLM only verbalises.
- **P-01 Privacy first** - no financial data leaves the device without explicit, revocable, per-feature consent.
- **P-02 Show the work** - every output shows its inputs + the rule/model that fired + a plain-language reason.
- **P-08 Deterministic & testable** - fixed input → fixed output; randomness only via an injected, seedable source.
- **AI-ARC-003/006** - every engine result carries provenance (engineId/version/inputWindow/computedAt/confidence/evidence).
