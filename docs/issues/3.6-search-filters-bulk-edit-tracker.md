## Strategy notes

**Schema v8 → v9.** FR-TXN-007's "tag" and FR-TXN-008's "retag" had no data model. SRS §20.1 names
`tags` and `transaction_tags`, so those are what landed — additive DDL, no backfill, with a
round-trip case in `MigrationRoundTripTest` that asserts the unique index as well as the rows.

**Paging 3.** FR-TXN-007's "infinite scroll via paging", via `androidx.paging` + `room-paging`. Two
consequences worth recording because neither is obvious from the requirement:

- **A transfer's two legs are now collapsed in SQL, not in Kotlin.** The old
  `List<Transaction>.toRows()` paired legs within a loaded day, which paging breaks the moment they
  land in different pages. The query drops the incoming leg and projects the counterpart account
  beside the outgoing one — except under an account filter, where the clause stands down so a user
  filtering to the *destination* account still sees the money arrive.
- **A day's total comes from its own `GROUP BY` query, never from the loaded rows.** A page boundary
  can fall inside a day, so a folded total would understate its own day until the user scrolled.

**The emulator gate earned its keep.** Two defects reached a running app with 1457 unit tests green:
a `cachedIn` placed after the `combine` (crash on the second visit) and an undo snackbar rendered
under the global FAB (the action unreachable). Neither was catchable by the unit suite — the first
because a fake `PagingData` is re-collectable where a real `Pager` is not, the second because no test
renders two feature modules' composables together. §9's "a green build does not close the issue" is
not ceremony.

**`observeRecent` was removed, not kept.** Its 30-day window was scaffolding whose own doc comment
named this issue as its removal. `TransactionsViewModel` was its only caller. The *upper* bound
survives as `TransactionFilter.boundedToActuals`, so a scheduled payment still stays out of the
actuals (FR-TXN-010) unless the filter explicitly names a future date.
