Design language · v2 proposal

One answer per screen

v1 put every true thing on the dashboard at once: net worth, Safe-to-Spend, budgets, insights, a forecast. All correct, all competing. v2 keeps the same engines, the same tokens and the same rules, and changes what the screen leads with — one figure, one sentence saying what it means, and the working one tap away. Six screens on demo data.

The figures on this page are illustrative. The clickable prototype computes everything from one seeded ledger, so its numbers are the consistent ones: docs/design/v2-prototype.html.

What changed, and why

Seven moves. None of them needs a new engine, a new dependency, or a new colour seed.

1 · One answer

The shipped dashboard already leads with Safe-to-Spend. What it then does is stack fourteen navigation rows — budgets, accounts, goals, vehicles, settings, export, refresh — at one weight. Those become five tabs, and the screen goes back to answering one question.

2 · A meaning line under every figure

“₹18,420.00” is data. “₹1,180 a day until 31 October, after rent, EMIs and your emergency floor” is an answer. The sentence is built from the same engine result, so it cannot disagree with the number above it.

3 · The work is one tap, not one screen

P-02 said show the work; v1 put it on a separate evidence screen almost nobody reached. Now it is an inline disclosure on the card itself: inputs, the rule id and version, the engine and when it ran.

4 · Pace is drawn, not described

One bar carries three facts: spent, the even-pace marker, and the overshoot in the warning tone. The projection stays in words, because “lands at ₹9,800 of ₹9,000” is the part a person acts on.

5 · Bigger targets, fewer of them

Add is a keypad and one primary button. Categories are 44px chips, not a dropdown. Every screen has exactly one primary action; secondary actions are text, so there is never a choice between two filled buttons.

6 · Trust is visible, not claimed

The status line says “offline · all local” on every screen. Consent rows show the grant and revoke dates. The erase states what it cannot reach. These are the app's main promises, so they belong in the chrome.

7 · Calmer surfaces, same seed

The trust-teal seed and every semantic role stay exactly as they are — this is not a repaint. What changes is restraint: 20px radii, one hairline instead of shadows, two surface levels, and amounts in tabular figures at a larger size than anything around them.

Rules this design does not get to break

Colour never signals alone

Every semantic colour is paired with a sign or a word, so it survives greyscale, colour-blindness and the privacy blur.

No figure without provenance within one tap

If a card shows a number, its disclosure names the rule and engine that produced it.

Every string is a resource

Including the meaning lines — they are translated into Hindi, Kannada and Tamil like everything else.

Nothing invents a number for the layout's sake

An empty state says it is empty. A blocked reply shows no figures at all.

Demo data in every mock and screenshot

The figures on this page are seeded, not anyone's money.
